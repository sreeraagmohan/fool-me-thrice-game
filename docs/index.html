About Fool Me Thrice

Every card in this game is real — even the fake ones. The FAKE claims are genuine misinformation that actually circulated on Indian social media, officially debunked by the government's fact-checking unit, PIB Fact Check. Nothing here is AI-invented fake news.

The REAL claims come from official PIB press releases, rewritten in the same casual register — so you can't judge by tone, only by judgment.

I built this because telling truth from viral fiction is a skill, and skills need practice. I write The Bharat Briefing, a newsletter on Indian government policy, business and geopolitics — this game is a side quest in the same mission.
